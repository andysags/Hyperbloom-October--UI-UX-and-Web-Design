# Demo film: storyboard

**File:** [`video/dose-film.mp4`](../video/dose-film.mp4) · 1920 × 1080 · 58 s · no voice-over (kinetic type + music, readable with the sound off)

**Approach:** 100 % code motion design. The film reuses Dose's real design system (Atkinson Hyperlegible, cream and indigo palette, woven-textile band) and the app's own SVG pill and pictogram drawings. It is rendered frame by frame in a headless browser and encoded with ffmpeg. Nothing is filmed from the screen, so every frame is sharp and true to the product.

**Honesty rules:** no invented numbers. The two figures on screen carry their source (UNESCO data 2022; Dowse & Ehlers 2005). The prescription shown is labelled *Example*.

**Music:** warm, optimistic piano (royalty-free, Mixkit licence). The musical lift lands when the patient's phone appears. Soft sound effects on clicks, ticks and pills.

| Time | Shot | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6 s | Hook | A prescription writes itself by hand: “4cp x2/j pdt repas x3j”. Then: **Can you read this?** |
| 6 to 11 s | Problem | “About half of adults in Benin can't read.” (source on screen) · “Prescriptions are handwritten, abbreviated, in French.” |
| 11 to 16 s | Logo | The two-tone capsule rolls in and becomes the logo. **Dose. The prescription everyone can read.** |
| 16 to 26 s | Pharmacist | Frequent medicines added in one tap, cards collapse into summaries, the voice message records, **Create** reveals the QR code. |
| 26 to 28 s | Hand-off | The QR code becomes the patient's phone. |
| 28 to 42 s | Patient | “Hello Afi · Day 2 of 3”. 4 pills pop one by one (*4 tablets = 4 drawings*), pill → plate, **I took it** turns green, the listen button speaks, the medicine sheet opens, the “only if needed” counter locks at 3 of 3. |
| 42 to 47 s | Paper | The phone becomes the printed sheet; boxes get ticked with a pen. |
| 47 to 52 s | Languages | *Morning → Matin → Mañana → Manhã → Mattina* · “+ the pharmacist's own voice in Fon, Yoruba, Mina…” |
| 52 to 58 s | End card | Logo, tagline, URL, “HyperBloom October 2026”. |
