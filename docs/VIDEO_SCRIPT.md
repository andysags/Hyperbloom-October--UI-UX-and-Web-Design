# Demo video: 2-minute script

## Notes en français (pour toi)

- **Durée cible : 1 min 50 à 2 min.** Le jury regarde beaucoup de vidéos : chaque seconde compte.
- **Voix off :** le texte ci-dessous est en anglais pour le jury. Tu peux aussi l'enregistrer en français et ajouter des **sous-titres anglais**, ce qui renforce l'authenticité.
- **Outils :** OBS Studio ou Loom pour filmer l'écran, et CapCut ou DaVinci Resolve pour monter et sous-titrer.
- **Avant de filmer :**
  1. Ouvre une **fenêtre de navigation privée**, pour repartir de zéro (aucune prise cochée).
  2. Pour la vue patient, active le mode téléphone de Chrome : touche F12, puis l'icône 📱, et choisis « iPhone 12 Pro ».
  3. Ajoute **`?lang=en&h=19`** à l'adresse : l'interface s'affiche en anglais et il est « 19 h », ce qui fait apparaître l'alerte « prise oubliée » et la prise du soir.
  4. Enregistre le **message vocal** avant de filmer, sur le même navigateur.
  5. Zoom du navigateur à 110 %, pour que tout soit lisible en vidéo.
- **Musique :** très basse, sans paroles (par exemple la bibliothèque audio de YouTube).

## Script

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 to 0:12** | Home page: zoom on the handwritten prescription (“4cp x2/j pdt repas x3j”). | *“This is what a prescription often looks like in Benin. Handwritten, abbreviated, in French. Now imagine you only speak Fon, and you can't read.”* |
| **0:12 to 0:25** | Scroll to the three problem cards. | *“The pharmacist explains it at the counter. Then the patient goes home and forgets. Wrong doses, missed doses, antimalarials stopped too early. Dose fixes that moment.”* |
| **0:25 to 0:50** | Pharmacist view. Type the name “Afi”, choose **Fɔngbè**, tap **Artemether-Lumefantrine**, **Paracetamol** and **Iron** in “Frequent”. Show the collapsed summaries, then open one card. The live preview updates on the right. | *“The pharmacist fills it in under a minute. Common medicines take one tap and arrive pre-filled, with the right shape, colour and times. On the right, they see exactly what the patient will see.”* |
| **0:50 to 1:00** | Step 4: click **Record**, then play back the voice message. Click **Create**: QR code, WhatsApp, SMS, Print. | *“Afi speaks Fon, so the pharmacist records a message in her language. Then: a QR code, WhatsApp, SMS, or paper.”* |
| **1:00 to 1:30** | Patient view on a phone, at 7 pm. Show “Hello Afi · Day 2 of 3”, the **missed dose** alert, then scroll to **Evening**: 4 drawn pills, plate → pill. Tap **I took it** (it turns green). Tap 🔊. | *“On her phone, Afi sees only today. Four tablets? Four drawings. Plate, then pill: after eating. One big button: I took it. She missed this morning, and Dose tells her what to do, safely. And she can listen to everything.”* |
| **1:30 to 1:42** | Tap **What is this medicine for?**: the sheet opens. Then show the “Only if needed” counter blocked at 3 of 3. | *“She can understand why to finish the treatment. And for painkillers, Dose stops her at the daily maximum.”* |
| **1:42 to 1:52** | Print preview of the paper sheet (front, then back), then the language selector switching to Español, Português and Italiano. | *“No smartphone? Print it: one box per dose. And the interface speaks five languages.”* |
| **1:52 to 2:00** | Back to the home page, logo, URL on screen. | *“Dose. The prescription everyone can read.”* |
